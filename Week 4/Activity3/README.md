## Customer - Use Case Diagram

The diagram models describe customer interactions with the Money Exchange System. The Customer actor can register an account, log in, check live exchange rates, convert currency, transfer money internationally, view transaction history, and download receipts.

## Bank Teller - Use Case Diagram
The diagram shows in-branch operations for the Money Exchange System. The Bank Teller actor verifies customer IDs, searches accounts, processes currency exchanges, collects or disburses cash, prints receipts, and processes transactions. The Customer actor initiates requests at the branch and receives services.
